# Personal Portfolio: Farrel Adel Mohammad

## Purpose
A professional personal portfolio website for Farrel Adel Mohammad, a Software Engineer specializing in Fullstack development and AI/LLM integration. The site serves as a digital business card and case study repository for clients and employers.

## Users
- Potential employers (CTOs, Engineering Managers)
- Clients in education, public services, and marine industries
- AI/Developer community

## Tone & Voice
- Intentional, restrained, and typographically driven.
- Calm and confident.
- Editorial (magazine or case study feel).
- Professional and minimalist.

## Anti-References
- Generic SaaS-style sections.
- Loud hierarchy jumps.
- Card-based UI with shadows.
- Bright accent colors or gradients.
- Overuse of animations.

## Strategic Principles
- Minimalist but not empty.
- Whitespace as structure.
- Monochrome palette only.
- Visual separation through lines and spacing, not cards.
- Left-aligned layout.
